# Thakurta spacetime

## Scope

The geometry is the non-rotating Thakurta family, conformal to Schwarzschild with a scale factor depending on static Schwarzschild time.
The expanding exterior has positive mass parameter and r greater than 2m.
The direction of the radial heat flow is stated with the scale-factor and observer assumptions.
Positive power laws and late-static regular-horizon families have separate causal conclusions.
