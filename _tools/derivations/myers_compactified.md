# Myers compactified black hole

## Sources

Gary T. Horowitz and Kengo Maeda, arXiv:hep-th/0201241v2, equation (2.7), give the five dimensional compactified metric in cylindrical coordinates.
James B. Gilmore, Andreas Ross, and Michael Smolkin, arXiv:0908.3490v2, equations (4.2)-(4.4), give its local spherical coordinates and the periodic potential.
Ryotaku Suzuki, Shunichiro Kinoshita, and Tetsuya Shiromizu, arXiv:1205.4596v1, equations (86)-(87), give the general dimensional periodic extremal Einstein-Maxwell solution.
Robert C. Myers, Physical Review D 35, 455-466 (1987), established the construction; the publisher's abstract and record were read, while the body was inaccessible.

## Coordinates and checks

Horowitz and Maeda's cylindrical coordinates cover one circle with $-L/2 ≤ z < L/2$.
The spherical chart uses $r = \rho\sin\chi$ and $z = \rho\cos\chi$, with $-L/2 < \rho\cos\chi < L/2$ to stay inside one copy of the circle.
The printer leaves $U$ and its derivatives as named functions of the coordinates, and its pointwise check substitutes the sourced function into the flat spatial Laplacian and the five dimensional Einstein-Maxwell equations.
Both charts printed their tensors under a 20 second per step budget, and both pointwise checks passed.

## Drawings

The two spacetime diagrams draw the same reflection plane $z = 0$ in the two sourced charts, with $\mu = 1$ and $L = 4\sqrt{\mu}$.
No conformal diagram is drawn: both sourced charts cover only the exterior, and the causal extension through the extremal horizon is not specified by these charts.
The embedding draws the equatorial reflection plane $t=z=0$ from $r=0.1\sqrt{\mu}$ to $4\sqrt{\mu}$, with the infinite throat and outer continuation identified at its edges.
The curvature maps read the published Kretschmann and Ricci scalars over one circle and $0 \le r \le 3\sqrt{\mu}$, at the same parameter values, without recomputing curvature.
