## The attraction

Write f=4(U-T) ln(r/r_0) and B=1-4(U+T) ln(r/r_0).
At release from rest the exact truncated radial equation is d²r/dt²=-Gamma^r_tt=-2(U-T)/(rB).
Its first-order part is -2(U-T)/r, also the negative radial derivative of Phi=f/2.
At U=T its numerator vanishes exactly.
Carter's equation of state UT=mu_0² permits U=alpha mu_0 and T=mu_0/alpha; alpha>1 makes the acceleration negative.

The first-order Levi-Civita match can be checked with rho=r[1+4T(1-ln(r/r_0))], m=2(U-T), and C=1+4T, after setting the radial unit r_0=1.
Pulling the Weyl metric back along this transformation recovers all four diagonal coefficients to first order.
The exact Ricci tensor of the truncated metric starts at second order.
Its higher-order value depends on the omitted higher-order metric terms and has no independently determined physical meaning at that order.
