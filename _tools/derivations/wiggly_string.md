# The averaged wiggly cosmic string

## Source and order

Camps and Emparan (2010), equation (2.8), is the cylindrical transverse gauge used here, in units G=c=1.
Its time and axial coefficients depend on U-T, and its transverse coefficient depends on U+T.
The tensors are exact tensors of this truncated line element, and the vacuum equations hold only through first order in U and T.
The source ledger and both approved prose readings are preserved in the task evidence directory.

## Coordinate audit

The cylindrical chart is the approved scope, as steering message 004 specifies.
The Cartesian expression is its ordinary polar-coordinate pullback, without a distinct physical region.
The Weyl expression of equations (2.5)-(2.7) differs by a coordinate transformation kept only to first order; its exact truncated tensors would describe a different higher-order completion.
Neither is added as a second chart.

## Exact exterior candidate

Ozdemir's exact construction includes transverse stresses and an electric current, so its source differs from the averaged string considered here.
The relation to Levi-Civita is m=2(U-T), sigma=m/2, C=1+4T through first order, not an exact matching of a string interior to its exterior.
