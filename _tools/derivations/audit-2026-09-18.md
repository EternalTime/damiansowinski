# What the checker disagrees with, and why

Written 18 September 2026, against the collection as it stood at commit 988193b.
`verify_metrics.py` reported 436 disagreements and 6 UNCHECKED over 16 coordinate systems, and that run is the baseline everything below is counted from.
This is a description of the shape of those 436, not a proposal to change any of them.
Three were fixed in the commit this file arrives with, and they are marked where they appear.

## The dimensional pass

`verify_metrics.py` now assigns a dimension to every coordinate, to $c$ and to every declared parameter, and checks that each term of each published expression carries the dimension its left hand side fixes.
The declarations live in `DIMENSIONS`, which replaced `TIME_COORDINATES`: a coordinate that carries dimensions of time is exactly a coordinate the chart multiplies by $c$, so the one table now gives both answers and they cannot drift apart.
The pass needs no algebra and takes about one second over the whole collection, so `--dimensions-only` is worth running on every edit; the full sympy check still takes about thirteen minutes.

Over the baseline it flagged 9 terms, standing for 8 published components, every one of which the sympy check also caught.
It is a filter, not a superset: a sign flip and a wrong value of the right dimension both pass it.

The other eight systems are clean.

**None of the 436 is the Ricci sign contraction.**
The checker contracted as $R_{\mu\nu} = R^\alpha{}_{\mu\nu\alpha}$ on the day this was written, which is what the files did, and on that reading no published Ricci tensor, Einstein tensor or Ricci scalar differed from sympy by a sign alone.
The Weyl group looked like it might be that convention leaking through, since the checker built Weyl from the other trace on purpose.
It is not: every one of the 320 was compared against the Weyl tensor built from the collection's own Ricci as well, and not a single component matched it.
The convention itself was settled the same day, in favour of the standard $R^\alpha{}_{\mu\alpha\nu}$, and the checker and every entry with a nonzero Ricci tensor were flipped onto it afterwards.
That pass negated published values and the checker together, so it moved no count in this table.

### 320: the Weyl block repeats the Riemann block

This is three quarters of the total and one mistake, made five times.
Every disagreeing published Weyl component is equal to the corresponding Riemann component of the same entry, checked component by component in both variants.

FRW and the interior Schwarzschild solution are conformally flat, so their Weyl tensors vanish identically, and the entries publish 48 nonzero components each.
`frw/comoving_spherical.weyl_tensor.llll` publishes $C_{trtr} = \dfrac{a\,\ddot{a}}{1-kr^2}$, which is the value its own `riemann` field carries, where sympy says $0$.

Godel and Lanczos-van Stockum are not conformally flat, so there the published Riemann values are wrong by the trace terms rather than wrong by everything.
`godel/cartesian.weyl_tensor.llll` publishes $C_{txtx} = \dfrac{1}{4\omega^2}$ against sympy's $\dfrac{1}{12\omega^2}$, and $\dfrac{1}{4\omega^2}$ is that entry's own $R_{txtx}$.
Reissner-Nordstrom is the mildest case: 16 of its 24 published components happen to be right, because there Riemann and Weyl agree in those slots, and the 8 that disagree are Riemann again.

Ellis-Bronnikov is the one entry whose published Weyl components are all correct; its 10 Weyl disagreements are omissions and are counted under Ellis-Bronnikov below.

### 50: nested radicals sympy will not combine

Not errors.
The interior Schwarzschild entry writes its radicals as $\sqrt{(R-r_s)(R^3-r^2r_s)}$ where sympy's own form is $R^2\sqrt{1-r_s/R}\,\sqrt{1-r^2r_s/R^3}$, and sympy will not merge the two without being told the factors are positive, so `norm` cannot drive the difference to zero.
`interior_schwarzschild/spherical.christoffel.ull ['t','t','r']` is the clearest: it is published as $\dfrac{r r_s}{-R^3 + r^2 r_s + 3R\sqrt{(R-r_s)(R^3-r^2r_s)}}$, which is exactly sympy's value, and the two agree to the last digit at every numerical point tried.
All 50 were checked numerically at two independent points and agreed.
They are the whole of that entry's Christoffel, Riemann, Ricci, Einstein and Ricci scalar disagreements.
Fixing them means teaching the reader or `norm` about positivity, not touching the physics.
That was done on 22 September 2026: `norm` now splits each radical into the square roots of the irreducible factors of its radicand, read as positive, and all 50 cancel.

### 41: the FRW Riemann

No single rule covers these, which is why they are listed apart from the Weyl group.
In `frw/comoving_spherical` the 24 published `ulll` components break down as 8 right, 6 equal to minus the right value, 6 equal to the corresponding fully lowered component, and 4 neither.
`['r','t','t','r']` is published as $\dfrac{a\,\ddot{a}}{1-kr^2}$, which is $R_{rttr}$, where $R^r{}_{ttr}$ is $\ddot{a}/a$.
The `llll` block publishes 12 of its 24 nonzero components, 10 of them negated, and leaves 12 out.
`ricci_tensor.uu` raises $R_{rr}$ with $g_{rr}$ rather than $g^{rr}$, so $R^{rr}$ comes out wrong by $(1-kr^2)^2$.
In `frw/conformal_spherical` only two components are wrong, both missing a factor $\sin^2\theta$.

Four of these are what the dimensional pass sees: $R^\theta{}_{tt\theta}$ has to carry $1/L^2$ and the published $r^2 a\,\ddot{a}$ carries nothing.

### 12: components Ellis-Bronnikov leaves out

Its `riemann.llll` publishes 2 fewer nonzero components than there are, and its `weyl_tensor.llll` publishes 14 of 24.
Every value it does publish is right.
`['\theta','t','t','\theta']` is missing from the Weyl block, where sympy says $-\dfrac{\ell^2}{3(\ell^2+r^2)}$.

### 8: the doubly raised Einstein tensor of the rotating dust entries

Godel and Lanczos-van Stockum both publish an Einstein tensor that agrees with sympy in the `ll` and `ul` variants and disagrees in `uu`, and in both the published `uu` block is not symmetric, which no Einstein tensor is.
Stockum publishes $G^{t\phi} = \dfrac{4e^{r^2/R^2}r^2(r-R)(r+R)}{R^5}$ and $G^{\phi t} = -\dfrac{4e^{r^2/R^2}}{R^3}$.
Godel publishes $G^{ty} = 8\omega^4\cosh x$ and $G^{yt} = -4e^{-x}\omega^4$.
Two of Stockum's four are also dimensional: $G^{t\phi}$ has to carry $1/L^3$ and the published value carries $1/L$.

### 2: the Rindler chart, fixed

The published $g_{TT} = -\dfrac{a^2X^2}{c^2}$ and $g^{TT} = -\dfrac{c^2}{a^2X^2}$ are the components of the bare $T$ chart, not of the chart $x^0 = cT$ every other value in the entry is printed in.
They carry $L^2/T^2$ and $T^2/L^2$ where a chart component of the metric carries nothing.
Corrected to $-\dfrac{a^2X^2}{c^4}$ and $-\dfrac{c^4}{a^2X^2}$, which is also what the entry's own $\Gamma_{TTX} = -\dfrac{a^2X}{c^4}$ requires.

### 2: a geodesic whose sign contradicts its own Christoffel symbol

Rindler published $\ddot{X} = \dfrac{a^2X}{c^4}\dot{T}^2$ beside $\Gamma^X{}_{TT} = \dfrac{a^2X}{c^4}$, and $\ddot{x}^\mu + \Gamma^\mu{}_{\nu\rho}\dot{x}^\nu\dot{x}^\rho = 0$ makes that a minus.
A free particle in the right Rindler wedge falls towards the horizon, so the sign matters physically.
Rewritten as $\ddot{X} + \dfrac{a^2X}{c^4}\dot{T}^2 = 0$, which is the form Kasner uses and the form the rest of the collection uses.

**Ellis-Bronnikov has the same defect and was left alone.**
It publishes $\ddot{r} = -r\dot{\theta}^2 - r\sin^2\theta\,\dot{\phi}^2$ beside $\Gamma^r{}_{\theta\theta} = -r$, which gives $\ddot{r} = +r\dot{\theta}^2 + r\sin^2\theta\,\dot{\phi}^2$.
Minkowski in spherical coordinates is the same equation with $\ell = 0$ and carries the opposite sign.

### 1: an inverse metric that does not invert the line element

`interior_schwarzschild/spherical` publishes
$$g^{tt} = -\frac{4}{\left(3\sqrt{1-\frac{r_s}{R^3}r^2} - \sqrt{1-\frac{r_s}{R}}\right)^2}$$
against a line element whose time term is $-\frac{1}{4}\left(\sqrt{1-\frac{r^2r_s}{R^3}} - 3\sqrt{1-\frac{r_s}{R}}\right)^2dt^2$.
The factor of 3 has moved to the other radical.
This is the one interior Schwarzschild disagreement that is real and is not the Weyl copy.

## The 6 UNCHECKED

Four are entries that publish nothing to check: `ellis_bronnikov` has no inverse metric, and `godel`, `interior_schwarzschild` and `rn_metric` have no geodesics.
Two are the interior Schwarzschild Einstein tensor and Kretschmann scalar, which sympy could not finish inside the 120 second budget; they are the same radicals that produced the 50 above.
Both finish inside the budget since the change to `norm` of 22 September 2026, and agree.

## What is worth deciding first

The Weyl group is 320 of 436 and one mistake, so it is the single cheapest thing to settle.
The interior Schwarzschild group is 50 of 436 and is a checker problem, not a physics problem.
Between them that is 85 per cent of the noise, and what is left is 66 disagreements over five entries, each of which needs reading on its own.

The collection now reports 433, which is the baseline with the three Minkowski lines gone and nothing else moved; the full run was diffed against the baseline line by line to be sure of that.
The dimensional pass reports 7 terms, all of them in the FRW Riemann and the Lanczos-van Stockum Einstein tensor.
