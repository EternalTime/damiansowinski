# What the checker disagrees with, and why

The baseline run of the checker is from 18 September 2026, at commit 988193b.
`verify_metrics.py` reported 436 disagreements and 6 UNCHECKED over 16 coordinate systems, and every count is taken against that run.
We describe the shape of those 436 and propose no change to any of them.
Three were fixed in the commit that followed that run, and they are marked fixed where they appear.

## The dimensional pass

`verify_metrics.py` now assigns a dimension to every coordinate, to $c$ and to every declared parameter, and checks that each term of each stated expression carries the dimension its left hand side fixes.
The declarations live in `DIMENSIONS`, which replaced `TIME_COORDINATES`: a coordinate that carries dimensions of time is exactly a coordinate the chart multiplies by $c$, so the one table now gives both answers and they cannot drift apart.
The pass needs no algebra and takes about one second over every spacetime, so `--dimensions-only` is worth running on every edit; the full sympy check still takes about thirteen minutes.

Over the baseline it flagged 9 terms, standing for 8 components, every one of which the sympy check also caught.
It is a filter, not a superset: a sign flip and a wrong value of the right dimension both pass it.

The other eight systems are clean.

**None of the 436 is the Ricci sign contraction.**
At the time of the run the Ricci tensor was contracted as $R_{\mu\nu} = R^\alpha{}_{\mu\nu\alpha}$, by the checker and in every stated component alike, and on that reading no stated Ricci tensor, Einstein tensor or Ricci scalar differed from sympy by a sign alone.
The Weyl group looked like it might be that convention leaking through, since the checker built Weyl from the other trace on purpose.
It is not: every one of the 320 was compared against the Weyl tensor built from the stated Ricci tensor as well, and not a single component matched it.
The convention itself was settled the same day, in favour of the standard $R^\alpha{}_{\mu\alpha\nu}$, and the checker and the Ricci tensor of every spacetime where it is nonzero were flipped onto it afterwards.
That change negated the stated components and the checker together, so it moved none of the counts.

### 320: the Weyl tensor repeats the Riemann tensor

This is three quarters of the total and one mistake, made five times.
Every disagreeing Weyl component is equal to the corresponding Riemann component of the same spacetime, checked component by component with the first index up and with every index down.

FRW and the interior Schwarzschild solution are conformally flat, so their Weyl tensors vanish identically, yet each was given 48 nonzero Weyl components.
In the comoving spherical chart of FRW the Weyl tensor with every index down had $C_{trtr} = \dfrac{a\,\ddot{a}}{1-kr^2}$, which is the value of its own Riemann component, where sympy says $0$.

Godel and Lanczos-van Stockum are not conformally flat, so there the copied Riemann values are wrong by the trace terms rather than wrong by everything.
In the Cartesian chart of Godel the Weyl tensor with every index down had $C_{txtx} = \dfrac{1}{4\omega^2}$ against sympy's $\dfrac{1}{12\omega^2}$, and $\dfrac{1}{4\omega^2}$ is that spacetime's own $R_{txtx}$.
Reissner-Nordstrom is the mildest case: 16 of its 24 stated Weyl components happen to be right, because there Riemann and Weyl agree in those slots, and the 8 that disagree are Riemann again.

Ellis-Bronnikov is the one spacetime whose stated Weyl components are all correct; its 10 Weyl disagreements are omissions and are counted with its other omissions.

### 50: nested radicals sympy will not combine

Not errors.
In the interior Schwarzschild components the radicals appear as $\sqrt{(R-r_s)(R^3-r^2r_s)}$ where sympy's own form is $R^2\sqrt{1-r_s/R}\,\sqrt{1-r^2r_s/R^3}$, and sympy will not merge the two without being told the factors are positive, so `norm` cannot drive the difference to zero.
The Christoffel symbol $\Gamma^t{}_{tr}$ of the spherical chart is the clearest: its stated value is $\dfrac{r r_s}{-R^3 + r^2 r_s + 3R\sqrt{(R-r_s)(R^3-r^2r_s)}}$, which is exactly sympy's value, and the two agree to the last digit at every numerical point tried.
All 50 were checked numerically at two independent points and agreed.
They are the whole of the interior Schwarzschild Christoffel, Riemann, Ricci, Einstein and Ricci scalar disagreements.
Fixing them means teaching the reader or `norm` about positivity, not touching the physics.
That was done on 22 September 2026: `norm` now splits each radical into the square roots of the irreducible factors of its radicand, read as positive, and all 50 cancel.

### 41: the FRW Riemann

No single rule covers these, which is why they stand apart from the Weyl group.
In the comoving spherical chart of FRW the 24 stated Riemann components with the first index up break down as 8 right, 6 equal to minus the right value, 6 equal to the corresponding fully lowered component, and 4 neither.
The component with upper index r and lower indices t, t and r was stated as $\dfrac{a\,\ddot{a}}{1-kr^2}$, which is $R_{rttr}$, where $R^r{}_{ttr}$ is $\ddot{a}/a$.
With every index down, 12 of the 24 nonzero Riemann components were stated, 10 of them negated, and 12 were left out.
The Ricci tensor with both indices up was raised from $R_{rr}$ with $g_{rr}$ rather than $g^{rr}$, so $R^{rr}$ comes out wrong by $(1-kr^2)^2$.
In the conformal spherical chart of FRW only two components are wrong, both missing a factor $\sin^2\theta$.

Four of these are what the dimensional pass sees: $R^\theta{}_{tt\theta}$ has to carry $1/L^2$ and the stated $r^2 a\,\ddot{a}$ carries nothing.

### 12: the missing Ellis-Bronnikov components

Its Riemann tensor with every index down lacks 2 of its nonzero components, and only 14 of the 24 nonzero components of its Weyl tensor with every index down were stated.
Every value that was stated is right.
The component $C_{\theta tt\theta}$ is missing, where sympy says $-\dfrac{\ell^2}{3(\ell^2+r^2)}$.

### 8: the doubly raised Einstein tensor of the rotating dust spacetimes

For Godel and Lanczos-van Stockum the stated Einstein tensor agrees with sympy with both indices down and with the first index up, and disagrees with both indices up, and in both spacetimes the stated tensor with both indices up is not symmetric, which no Einstein tensor is.
Stockum's stated components are $G^{t\phi} = \dfrac{4e^{r^2/R^2}r^2(r-R)(r+R)}{R^5}$ and $G^{\phi t} = -\dfrac{4e^{r^2/R^2}}{R^3}$.
Godel's are $G^{ty} = 8\omega^4\cosh x$ and $G^{yt} = -4e^{-x}\omega^4$.
Two of Stockum's four are also dimensional: $G^{t\phi}$ has to carry $1/L^3$ and the stated value carries $1/L$.

### 2: the Rindler chart, fixed

The stated $g_{TT} = -\dfrac{a^2X^2}{c^2}$ and $g^{TT} = -\dfrac{c^2}{a^2X^2}$ are the components of the bare $T$ chart, not of the chart $x^0 = cT$ that every other Rindler component is written in.
They carry $L^2/T^2$ and $T^2/L^2$ where a chart component of the metric carries nothing.
They were corrected to $-\dfrac{a^2X^2}{c^4}$ and $-\dfrac{c^4}{a^2X^2}$, which is also what Rindler's own $\Gamma_{TTX} = -\dfrac{a^2X}{c^4}$ requires.

### 2: a geodesic whose sign contradicts its own Christoffel symbol

The Rindler geodesic equation read $\ddot{X} = \dfrac{a^2X}{c^4}\dot{T}^2$ beside $\Gamma^X{}_{TT} = \dfrac{a^2X}{c^4}$, and $\ddot{x}^\mu + \Gamma^\mu{}_{\nu\rho}\dot{x}^\nu\dot{x}^\rho = 0$ makes that a minus.
A free particle in the right Rindler wedge falls towards the horizon, so the sign matters physically.
It was rewritten as $\ddot{X} + \dfrac{a^2X}{c^4}\dot{T}^2 = 0$, the form the geodesic equations of Kasner and of every other spacetime take.

**The Ellis-Bronnikov geodesic equation has the same defect and was left alone.**
It reads $\ddot{r} = -r\dot{\theta}^2 - r\sin^2\theta\,\dot{\phi}^2$ beside $\Gamma^r{}_{\theta\theta} = -r$, which gives $\ddot{r} = +r\dot{\theta}^2 + r\sin^2\theta\,\dot{\phi}^2$.
Minkowski in spherical coordinates is the same equation with $\ell = 0$ and carries the opposite sign.

### 1: an inverse metric that does not invert the line element

In the spherical chart of the interior Schwarzschild star the inverse metric had
$$g^{tt} = -\frac{4}{\left(3\sqrt{1-\frac{r_s}{R^3}r^2} - \sqrt{1-\frac{r_s}{R}}\right)^2}$$
against a line element whose time term is $-\frac{1}{4}\left(\sqrt{1-\frac{r^2r_s}{R^3}} - 3\sqrt{1-\frac{r_s}{R}}\right)^2dt^2$.
The factor of 3 has moved to the other radical.
This is the one interior Schwarzschild disagreement that is real and is not the Weyl copy.

## The 6 UNCHECKED

Four had nothing stated to check: no inverse metric for Ellis-Bronnikov, and no geodesic equations for Godel, the interior Schwarzschild star and Reissner-Nordstrom.
Two are the interior Schwarzschild Einstein tensor and Kretschmann scalar, which sympy could not finish inside the 120 second budget; they are the same radicals that produced the 50 nested radical disagreements.
Both finish inside the budget since the change to `norm` of 22 September 2026, and agree.

## What is worth deciding first

The Weyl group is 320 of 436 and one mistake, so it is the single cheapest thing to settle.
The interior Schwarzschild group is 50 of 436 and is a checker problem, not a physics problem.
Between them that is 85 per cent of the noise, and what is left is 66 disagreements over five spacetimes, each of which needs reading on its own.

The checker now reports 433, which is the baseline with the three Minkowski lines gone and nothing else moved; the full run was diffed against the baseline line by line to be sure of that.
The dimensional pass reports 7 terms, all of them in the FRW Riemann and the Lanczos-van Stockum Einstein tensor.
