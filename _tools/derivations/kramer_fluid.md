# Kramer's bounded rotating fluid

Chinea and Pareja, arXiv:gr-qc/9909048v1, equation (1), reproduce the 1984 geometry.
Their equations (2)-(4) fix the fluid equation of state and stress.
The square root coordinate $w=\sqrt{\eta-1}$ is introduced after equation (22), and equation (59) confirms its meridional metric.

## Sources and domain
