# Radiating spacetime with a positive cosmological constant

## Sources and coordinates

The outgoing null chart appears in Campos, Molina, and Lima (2025), Eq. (2).
The ingoing null chart appears in Senovilla (2023), Eq. (2).

## Geometry

The outgoing metric has $g_{ur}=-1$ and $m=m(u)$, while the ingoing metric has $g_{vr}=1$ and $m=m(v)$.
The positive roots of $1-2m/r-\Lambda r^2/3=0$ are distinct for $0<9\Lambda m^2<1$, merge for $9\Lambda m^2=1$, and are absent for $9\Lambda m^2>1$.
The mass function determines the null flux, with opposite signs for the two orientations.

## Drawings

The null diagrams use smooth local mass histories $m(v)=1/2+v/50$ and $m(u)=1/2-u/50$, with $\Lambda=1/20$ in length units.
The ingoing equatorial slice $v-r=0$ is embedded from $r=0.1$ to $r=8$ for that same history.
A conformal diagram is not drawn: the mass history is free and its global completion and event horizons are unspecified.
Senovilla (2023), Figures 1 and 2, gives collapse histories with different causal boundaries on opposite sides of the mass threshold.
The unbounded linear mass used in the local null plots is not assumed to be a global solution with nonnegative mass.

The curvature maps evaluate the stored scalars over $0<r<8$ and $-4<u<4$, using the outgoing local mass history.

## Checks

The chart generator compares the metric and its inverse at two points.
The dedicated test reads the published Ricci tensor and metric components to check the null flux, and checks the subcritical, degenerate, and supercritical threshold.
