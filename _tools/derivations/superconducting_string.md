The metric is the far exterior of a straight, stationary string.
Eiroa and Simeone, arXiv:gr-qc/0404050, equations 36-39, give the cylindrical line element and magnetic field in units $c=G=1$.
The current and electromagnetic field use Gaussian units, with $\nabla_\mu F^{\mu\nu}=4\pi j^\nu$.
Sen's arXiv:gr-qc/9808075, equations 17-18, has the same geometry after the parameter and scale identification recorded in the research ledger; his current uses a different electromagnetic normalization.
The exterior begins beyond the core, where the electromagnetic stress dominates the other string fields.
