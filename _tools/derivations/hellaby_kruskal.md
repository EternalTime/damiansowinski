# A Kruskal universe of dust

## Sources and chart

Hellaby and Krasiński's 2002 paper, equations 1, 4, 11, 12, 16, 20, and 225-227, supplies the comoving spherical chart and the explicit mirror symmetric example.
Its section 8.3.4 calls test 2 the Lemaître-Tolman case.
The journal record and abstract of Hellaby 1987 establish the earlier dense two sheet model and its priority, while the published full text is unread.

## The neck

At $r=0$, $f=-1$ and $R_r=0$.
The source's regularity conditions hold the limit of $R_r^2/(1+f)$ finite.
For $f=-e^{-r^2/r_s}$, the limit is $r_s R_{rr}(t,0)^2$, provided the ordinary regularity conditions hold.
The Tolman-Bondi chart in the collection requires $1+2E>0$ and $R_r>0$ and therefore excludes the neck itself.
With $f=2E$, the condition $1+2E=0$ is necessary for this regular extremum but does not uniquely pick the model.

## Checks

The chart's tensors are to be printed and checked against the dust equations at points away from the removable neck limit.

## Drawings

The spatial embedding follows Hellaby and Buckley 2021, equations 21-25.

The cited 2002 example specifies the neck and local evolution, while its full conformal boundary is not established there.
The mass function grows without bound as $|r|$ grows, so a diagram for an asymptotically empty model cannot be assumed for this example.
The conformal diagram is not drawn until its global boundary is determined.
