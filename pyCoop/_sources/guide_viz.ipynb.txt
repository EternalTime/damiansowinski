    "\n",
    "A cooperation game is a story - players drifting, wavering inside the change-of-heart bands, flipping, and finally locking in - and stories are easier to follow when you can see them.\n",
    "pyCoop draws states, traces, and films, all from the trajectory object.\n",
    "\n",
    "## Snapshots\n",

    "Actions color the nodes over a diverging map from $-1$ to $+1$, and opinions set the border, dark for $+1$ and light for $-1$, so a node whose fill and border disagree is a player acting against their belief:"
