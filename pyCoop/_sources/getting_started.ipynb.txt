    "from pycoop import observables as obs\n",
    "\n",
    "game = pc.Game(pc.paw(), u=0.5, l=0.2, T=1.0)\n",

    "Three parameters govern everything.\n",
    "The agreement threshold `u` lets a player reconsider only while their local mean action lies inside $(-u, u)$, and the position threshold `l` lets them do it only once committed, $|\\sigma_j| > l$, which keeps fence-sitters from flipping.\n",
    "The third, `T`, sets the tempo: the mean wait between decision events across the network is $T/N$.\n",
    "See [The Dynamics](guide_dynamics.ipynb) for the rules in full.\n",
    "\n",
